authorize a tag, publication, production signature, registry publication, or P4
closure.**

`1.0.0` may be published while P4-1 through P4-6 remain open. It is a proposal
only. The P4 status remains `NOT CLOSED` until the authoritative checklist and
evidence JSON are updated through the approved governance process.

## Accepted risks

| ID | Risk | Impact if accepted | Compensating control | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P4-1 | No approved production signing identity or key custody | Users cannot verify a production signature; artifact authenticity depends on checksums and the source repository | Local SHA-256 manifest and SBOM; explicit unsigned status in the attestation record; no claim of production signing | Artifact origin remains only as trustworthy as the distribution channel |
| P4-2 | Registry ownership, 2FA/recovery, and clean-install evidence are absent | No package channel is authorized; an unofficial channel could confuse users | Do not publish to registries; document the absence of channel authorization; keep packaging metadata local | Users may install from an unofficial or unverified source |
| P4-3 | No clean-runner release evidence | Reproducibility and runner isolation are unproven | Build locally with `--locked`; record commands, toolchain, and hashes; do not claim clean-runner attestation | A compromised or undeclared local dependency could affect artifacts |
| P4-4 | No published build-provenance or SBOM attestations | Provenance and SBOM association cannot be independently verified from a published release | Generate and checksum the local CycloneDX SBOM; state that no OIDC attestations exist | SBOM and provenance trust remain unproven |
| P4-5 | No independent OT/ICS security assessment | OT/ICS-specific security claims are not independently validated | Do not market the release as OT/ICS-assessed; preserve the assessment as a blocking requirement | Unknown OT/ICS-specific weaknesses may remain |
| P4-6 | No named reviewer sign-off or completed formal review | The release has not received formal P4 approval | Keep the P4 checklist open; record this draft as unapproved; require named approval before publication | Governance and documentation defects may remain |
| Platform | Windows Npcap raw-SYN runtime is unverified | Windows raw features may fail or behave differently | Document the limitation; require verification or a separate explicit waiver before publication | Windows raw-SYN users may encounter unsupported behavior |
| Platform | Full-range raw SYN parity on PingPong is unresolved | Full-range completeness is not established for that environment | Document the 1/15 full-range result and the 15/15 cooldown result; do not claim parity | Users may overestimate full-range reliability in similar environments |

## Conditions

1. This draft must not be used to tag, push, publish, or promote `1.0.0`.
2. No production GPG or cosign key may be represented as approved custody.
3. No registry, container, or package publication may occur under this draft.
4. The P4 checklist and evidence JSON must continue to report `NOT CLOSED`.
5. Any artifact rebuild, version change, or source change invalidates this draft
   and requires a new review.
6. Approval must name the approvers, scope, date, and expiry. An unsigned or
   incomplete approval is invalid.
7. The risk acceptance expires on the recorded expiry date. It does not renew
   automatically.
8. Publication after approval still requires the release preflight, final
   artifact verification, and the user's explicit publication authorization.
